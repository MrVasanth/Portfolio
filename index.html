What I've learned

I don't optimize
campaigns. I optimize
buying journeys.

Tools change. Channels change. Algorithms change. The thing that stays constant is the buyer trying to decide if you're worth a meeting. Everything I do is engineered around that decision.

I'd rather spend $80K on 4,000 qualified clicks than $40K on 40,000 cheap ones. Volume is a vanity metric. Intent is a leading indicator of pipeline.

Rising CAC usually starts on the landing page, I fix the page first. Spend scales after the page earns it.

If a dashboard doesn't change where the next dollar goes, it isn't doing its job.

The best campaigns don't need a clever idea. Search terms and Sales calls tell you what to build next. You just have to listen.

A good system keeps delivering long after a clever campaign is forgotten. It scales without you in the room.
